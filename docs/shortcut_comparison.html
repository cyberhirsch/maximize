Left hand or not?

The mouse sits in the right hand, so keys under the left hand can be pressed without letting go of it. The left-hand zone is Esc F1–F4 ` 1–5 Q–T A–G Z–B plus Tab, Caps, left Shift/Ctrl/Alt and Space. Distance is measured in key widths from the nearest of A S D F. Modifiers are not counted here.
